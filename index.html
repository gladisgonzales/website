Gladis Gonzales

Assistant Professor, Loyola Andalucía University
Member of LoyolaBehLab

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Quantitative Methods at Universidad Loyola Andalucía, a position I have held since July 2025, and a researcher at the Loyola Behavioral Lab (LoyolaBehLab). My research focuses on creativity, homophily and economic preferences among adolescents, combining behavioral experiments with social network data. I earned my PhD in Behavioral Economics within the Data Science doctoral program at Universidad Loyola Andalucía, under the supervision of Prof. Pablo Brañas and Prof. Antonio Alfonso, after completing an MRes in Quantitative Social Sciences at the same university. I also hold a degree in Systems Engineering from the Catholic University of Honduras. Before my academic career, I coordinated impact evaluations and fieldwork in Honduras and El Salvador. I have experience running behavioral experiments with adolescents in diverse social contexts, from research design and field coordination to data processing and statistical analysis.
